Loss Aversion Copy
People feel losses 2× more intensely than gains. Every copy should frame what you'll MISS.
Current: "The energy is real. The experience is unique."
Better: "120 people will be at Bethel Lozana July 18. The question is whether you're one of them."
Decoy Pricing Order
$249 Premium is the decoy — makes both $35 and $419 look rational.
WRONG ORDER → GA $35 · Premium $249 · Luxury $419
RIGHT ORDER → Luxury $419 [RECOMMENDED] · Premium $249 · GA $35
